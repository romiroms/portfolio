About

I’m a second-year BSc Marine Environmental Science student at the University of Mauritius, and I’m a beginner in GIS who is still learning. This portfolio collects my maps as I build my skills, from raw data and shapefiles in QGIS to finished, interactive layers.

My interest in GIS began during my internship at the Mauritius Oceanography Institute, where I worked with GPS, GIS and beach profiling for coastal monitoring.
